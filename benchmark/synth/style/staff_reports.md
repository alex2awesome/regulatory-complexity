# Style: council staff reports, agenda items, action minutes, SOPs (distilled from Hayward Legistar items, HCD review letters, real SOP excerpts)

**Staff report.** Header: "DATE / TO: Mayor and City Council / FROM: Director of Development Services / SUBJECT: Adopt an Ordinance Establishing…"; sections **Recommendation** (one sentence: "That the City Council adopt…"), **Summary**, **Background** (the state law, prior local program, dates), **Discussion** (this is where designed-tier facts live: proposed workflow in prose — "Applications will be received at the Permit Center, screened for completeness within five business days, and routed to a Building Inspector and, where the unit is in a hazard zone, to the Fire Prevention Bureau…"; staffing ("0.5 FTE Permit Technician"); expected volumes ("staff anticipates 40–60 applications in the first year"); comparison with neighboring cities — the template-lineage hook), **Fiscal Impact** (fee levels, cost recovery, General Fund), **Strategic Roadmap / Sustainability**, **Public Contact**, **Next Steps**, **Attachments** (draft ordinance, fee resolution, comparison table). 800–1,800 words. Staff reports reveal *some* back-office steps (routing, sign-off levels) but rarely durations per step.

**Action minutes.** Terse: item number, title, "Motion to adopt by Council Member X, seconded by Y; carried 5-0"; public comment speakers by name and stance; occasionally a Council question that reveals a process fact ("Council Member Q asked how long the third-party inspection option takes; the Director responded that…").

**SOP / procedure manual (staff-only; rarely public).** Numbered steps with role, system, and artifact: "3.2 The Permit Technician creates the case in TRAKiT (case type AMN), attaches the intake checklist, collects the deposit, and routes to the Senior Building Inspector queue. 3.3 The Senior Inspector reviews within 3 business days…". Includes screens/queues, escalation rules ("if the applicant does not respond within 30 days, close as inactive and refer to Code Enforcement"), and quality checks (second reviewer, supervisor approval of denials). 600–1,500 words. This is the document that reveals `staff_only` activities — when the world publishes one (via an audit or records request), it is the ceiling-raising artifact.

**Audit excerpt.** "Figure 3: The X process" described in prose with stage durations from case files ("the median time from intake to first inspection was 34 days; 20 percent of cases exceeded 90 days"), findings and recommendations.

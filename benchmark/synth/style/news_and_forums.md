# Style: local news, council-watch blogs, non-reddit forums

**Local news** (a small daily or Patch-like outlet): dateline, lede on the *human* story or the council vote, a quote from the department director, one from an applicant or advocate, numbers ("the city has received 112 applications and issued 31 certificates"; "fees of $570"), a paragraph on the state law that created the program (bill number, author, year), and a closing on what's next. 400–800 words. News reveals volumes, backlog, fee, and occasionally a back-office bottleneck ("applications are routed to a single inspector").

**Forums / Q&A** (city-data, Quora, contractor forums, Nextdoor-like): shorter than reddit, older, more polite; threads often drift across jurisdictions ("In Lafayette they required…", "my cousin in Corvane…") — a natural contamination carrier.

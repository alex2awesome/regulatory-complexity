# Style: statutes and local ordinances (distilled from Cal. Gov. Code § 66311.7, HSC § 17920.3, city ordinances/resolutions)

**Statute.** Header: Code name, Title/Division/Chapter/Article headings with bracketed section ranges and history notes ("( Chapter 13 added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 7, Sec. 20. )"). Then the section number and lettered subdivisions (a)(b)(c)… with numbered paragraphs (1)(2). Register: "Notwithstanding any other law…", "a local agency shall not deny…", "may deny … if the local agency makes a finding that…", "This section shall not apply to…", "A local agency shall inform the public about…", definitions by reference ("as defined in Section 17920.3"), and explicit time limits ("within 60 days of receipt of a complete application"). Optional elements use "may"; mandated elements use "shall". Statutes leave implementation open in recognizable ways: "The fee shall not exceed the reasonable cost of…", "The local agency may adopt an ordinance to implement…", silence on which department. 300–900 words. Include a `Legislative history` line and an effective date.

**Local ordinance.** "ORDINANCE NO. 24-118 — AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HALVERN AMENDING CHAPTER 17.40 OF THE HALVERN MUNICIPAL CODE TO ESTABLISH…"; recitals ("WHEREAS, Assembly Bill 2533 (Stats. 2024, ch. 7) requires…"; "WHEREAS, the City wishes to…"), then "NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL … DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:", Section 1 Findings, Section 2 code amendment with the new municipal-code sections (definitions, applicability, application requirements, fees "as set by resolution of the City Council", inspection, decision, appeal "to the Hearing Officer within 10 days"), Section 3 CEQA, Section 4 Severability, Section 5 Effective date (30 days), and an adoption block (introduced/adopted dates, AYES/NOES roll call, Mayor/City Clerk signatures). 500–1,200 words.

**Resolution (fee).** "RESOLUTION NO. 25-031 … ESTABLISHING FEES FOR…"; recitals about cost-recovery studies; a fee table (Service | Fee | Basis) — the ONE place fees are exact; supersedes language ("Resolution No. 23-088 is hereby rescinded") — the prior-version hook.

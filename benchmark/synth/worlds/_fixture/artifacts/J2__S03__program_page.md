# Unpermitted Unit Compliance Program

The Planning Department administers Ardenmoor's compliance program for existing unpermitted dwelling units under Marisol Government Code section 41210 and City Council Resolution No. 2024-32. The program applies to units constructed before January 1, 2020.

## Steps

1. **Submit the application.** File Form P-51 with existing-condition plans and the $275 application fee online or at the Planning counter, 90 Fairmont Street. No appointment is needed.
2. **Completeness review.** Planning staff notify you within 30 days whether the application is complete.
3. **Code review.** A planner reviews the application with the Building Official. Corrections, if any, are emailed to the applicant.
4. **Inspection.** You may choose either a city inspection at no additional charge, or an inspection by a licensed third-party inspector from the city's approved list at your own expense. Third-party reports are accepted if submitted on Form P-53 within 30 days of the inspection.
5. **Certificate of Compliance.** Planning issues a Certificate of Compliance when all corrections are cleared.

## Timeline

Applicants should expect 30 to 45 days from a complete application to a certificate. Choosing the third-party inspection option typically saves one to two weeks because city inspection slots are limited.

## Fee waiver

The application fee is waived for applicants at or below 80 percent of area median income. Submit Form P-49 with the application.

CITY OF HALVERN — CITY COUNCIL STAFF REPORT
Meeting date: March 11, 2025            Item 7
From: R. Okonkwo, Chief Building Official
Subject: Adoption of Ordinance No. 24-118 and amendment of the master fee schedule for the
unit legalization program

RECOMMENDATION
Adopt Ordinance No. 24-118, transfer program administration from Planning to the Building
Division, and set the application fee at $430 and the inspection fee at $185.

BACKGROUND AND CURRENT WORKFLOW
The program has operated since 2022 under Ordinance No. 22-041 with a $390 application fee.
Planning accepted applications; Building performed plan review. The split intake is the primary
source of delay identified in the Division's file review of 143 applications closed in FY 2023-24:

1. Counter intake and screening by Permit Center staff (median 4 calendar days).
2. Interdepartmental routing. Every application is circulated to the Fire Prevention Bureau and,
   where the unit is served by a private lateral, to Public Works for utility review. This step is
   not visible to applicants and adds a median of 9 days; 12 percent of files wait more than
   three weeks for Fire comment.
3. Plan review by a plans examiner (median 21 days to first comment). 35 percent of applications
   receive a correction notice and re-enter plan review after resubmittal.
4. Field inspection (median 11 days from request to inspection).
5. Second-level sign-off. The Chief Building Official reviews and signs every legalization
   approval. With one incumbent this step has queued at a median of 5 days and up to 18 days
   during vacation coverage.
6. Fee reconciliation by Finance before the certificate is released; understated inspection fees
   were invoiced late in 31 of 143 files.
7. Records archiving to the permanent parcel file (post-issuance, clerical).

PERFORMANCE
Median time from submittal to issuance, FY 2023-24: 47 calendar days. 90th percentile: 96 days.
Denial rate: 8 percent (11 of 143), almost all for units that could not meet egress or ceiling
height. Staff estimate the ordinance will reduce median time by 6 to 9 days by consolidating
intake and authorizing the plans examiner to sign routine approvals when the Chief Building
Official is unavailable.

FISCAL IMPACT
Full cost recovery requires $615 per application (3.5 FTE allocated, $212,000 program cost,
FY 2023-24 volume of 143). Staff recommend $430 plus $185 at inspection.

# Unit legalization: frequently asked questions

*Page last reviewed November 2024.*

**Who runs the program?**
The Planning Division accepts and screens unit legalization applications. Planning staff will route your application to Building for plan review.

**What does it cost?**
The application fee is $390. There is a separate inspection fee. Fees are set in the city's master fee schedule and are updated each fiscal year.

**Do I need an appointment before applying?**
No. You may submit a completed application at the Permit Center during counter hours, or mail it to the Planning Division.

**How long does it take?**
Plan on about 30 days from submittal to a decision if your application is complete and the unit does not need corrections.

**What if my unit was built after 2020?**
The amnesty program does not apply. You would need a standard building permit for the conversion, and the unit may be subject to code enforcement.

**Will the city report me to code enforcement if I apply?**
No. Marisol Government Code section 41210(h) bars the city from initiating an enforcement action based solely on an application filed under the amnesty program, unless the unit presents an immediate hazard.

**Can I sell the house while the application is pending?**
Yes, but the Certificate of Legalization is issued to the property, not the owner, and any outstanding corrections transfer with the parcel.

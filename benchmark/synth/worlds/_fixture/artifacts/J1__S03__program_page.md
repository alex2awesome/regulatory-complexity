# Unpermitted Dwelling Unit Legalization Program

The Building Division administers Halvern's unit legalization program under Marisol Government Code section 41210 and Halvern Ordinance No. 24-118. The program lets an owner bring an existing unpermitted dwelling unit into compliance without the unit being red-tagged, provided the unit was constructed before January 1, 2020 and can be brought up to the standards in Chapter 15.42 of the Municipal Code.

## How to apply

1. **Schedule a pre-application meeting.** A pre-application meeting with a Building Division plans examiner is required before an application will be accepted. Meetings are held Tuesdays and Thursdays at the Permit Center, 411 Ridgeway Ave., 2nd floor. Call (555) 214-8890 or use the online scheduler.
2. **Submit Form BLD-214** with existing-condition floor plans, a site plan, and the legalization application fee of $430. Applications are accepted at the Permit Center counter or through the online portal.
3. **Application screening.** Permit Center staff review your submittal for completeness. Within 30 days of submittal you will receive either a notice that the application is complete or a completeness letter listing what is missing.
4. **Plan review.** A plans examiner reviews the application against the Building Code and Chapter 15.42. Most applicants receive one round of corrections. Corrected plans are resubmitted through the portal and re-enter plan review.
5. **Site inspection.** A building inspector inspects the unit. The inspection fee is $185, billed before the inspection is scheduled. Reinspection, if required, is $95.
6. **Certificate of Legalization.** When plan review and inspection are cleared, the Building Division issues a Certificate of Legalization for the unit.

## How long does it take?

Most complete applications are resolved in six to eight weeks from submittal. Applications that require corrections take longer. The 30-day period in step 3 is a completeness determination only; it is not a deadline for the final decision.

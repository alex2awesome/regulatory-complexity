# City of Halvern — Master Fee Schedule FY 2025-26 (excerpt)

Adopted by Resolution No. 25-07; effective April 1, 2025. All fees are cost-recovery under Marisol Government Code section 41210(f), which provides that a fee adopted under this article shall not exceed the reasonable cost of providing the service.

## Building Division

| Service | Fee | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unit legalization application (Ch. 15.42) | $430.00 | Due at submittal; nonrefundable once screening begins |
| Unit legalization inspection | $185.00 | Billed before inspection is scheduled |
| Reinspection (each) | $95.00 | Charged when a reinspection is required |
| Pre-application meeting | No charge | Required for unit legalization |
| Plan review, third and subsequent submittal | $140.00 | Per resubmittal after the second |
| Residential building permit, minor alteration | $268.00 | |
| Records research request | $52.00 | Per address |

Fee waivers: an applicant whose household income is at or below 80 percent of county median income may request a waiver of the legalization application fee on Form FIN-9. Waivers do not cover inspection or reinspection fees.

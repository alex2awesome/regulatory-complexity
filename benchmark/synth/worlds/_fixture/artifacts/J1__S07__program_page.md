# Sidewalk Vending Permits

Community Services issues sidewalk vending permits under Marisol Government Code section 51422 and Halvern Municipal Code Chapter 5.28. A permit is required to sell food or merchandise from a stationary or roaming cart on a public sidewalk.

## Steps

1. Complete Form CS-31 and attach a photo of the cart, a copy of your Marisol seller's permit, and, for food carts, a county health permit from Marisol County Environmental Health.
2. Pay the annual permit fee of $125 ($85 on renewal).
3. Cart inspection at the Community Services yard, 2120 Del Norte Road. Inspections are scheduled within 10 business days of a complete application.
4. Permit issuance. Staff issue the decal and the permit card at the counter.

Most complete applications are issued within 15 business days. Enforcement of vending violations is by administrative citation only; sidewalk vending is not a criminal offense in Halvern.

Food carts must also hold a Marisol County health permit before the city permit will be issued. The county process is separate and takes longer than the city process; start with the county.

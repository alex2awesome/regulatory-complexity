ORDINANCE NO. 24-118

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF HALVERN ADDING CHAPTER 15.42 TO THE
HALVERN MUNICIPAL CODE RELATING TO THE LEGALIZATION OF EXISTING UNPERMITTED DWELLING
UNITS, AND AMENDING THE MASTER FEE SCHEDULE

Adopted March 11, 2025. Effective April 1, 2025. Supersedes Ordinance No. 22-041.

The City Council of the City of Halvern ordains as follows:

SECTION 1. FINDINGS. Marisol Government Code section 41210 authorizes a city to establish a
program for the legalization of dwelling units constructed without permits before January 1,
2020, and requires each participating city to designate an administering department, publish a
submittal checklist, determine the completeness of an application within 30 days, and provide
an appeal of a denial to the governing body.

SECTION 2. CHAPTER 15.42 ADDED.

15.42.030 Administering department. The Building Division shall administer this chapter.
Applications previously accepted by the Planning Division shall be transferred to the Building
Division upon the effective date of this ordinance.

15.42.040 Pre-application meeting. An applicant shall attend a pre-application meeting with a
plans examiner before an application is accepted. The Building Official shall publish meeting
availability and may waive the meeting in writing where the unit has a current, active building
permit history.

15.42.050 Completeness determination. Within 30 days of submittal the Building Division shall
notify the applicant in writing that the application is complete, or shall identify the items
required to complete it. Resubmittal restarts the 30-day period.

15.42.060 Inspection. The Building Division shall inspect the unit. Where the inspection
identifies noncompliance, the Division shall issue a correction notice and the applicant may
obtain permits to perform the corrections; the unit shall be reinspected upon completion.

15.42.080 Fees. The application fee and the inspection fee shall be as set in the master fee
schedule and shall not exceed the reasonable cost of administering this chapter.

15.42.090 Appeal. An applicant may appeal a denial to the City Council within 15 days of the
notice of denial.

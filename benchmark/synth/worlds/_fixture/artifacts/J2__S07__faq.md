# Sidewalk vending: frequently asked questions

**Do I need a permit to vend on an Ardenmoor sidewalk?**
Yes. Ardenmoor issues sidewalk vending permits through the Business Services counter under Marisol Government Code section 51422.

**What does it cost?**
$95 per year. There is no inspection fee. Food carts must hold a county health permit.

**How long does it take?**
Business Services issues permits within 10 business days of a complete application. There is no hearing and no plan review.

**Where may I not vend?**
Within 100 feet of a school entrance during school hours, in the Fairmont Street farmers market footprint on Saturdays, or in a park where a concession agreement is in effect.

**Is there a cap on the number of permits?**
No. State law prohibits a cap that is not based on objective health, safety, or welfare concerns.

**Can my permit be revoked?**
Yes, after a fourth administrative citation in a twelve-month period. Revocation may be appealed to the City Manager.

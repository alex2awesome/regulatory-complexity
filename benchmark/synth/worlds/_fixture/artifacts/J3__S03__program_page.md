# Unpermitted Structure Legalization (unincorporated county)

The Planning and Development Department administers legalization of unpermitted dwelling units and accessory structures in the unincorporated area under Marisol Government Code section 41210 and County Code Title 22, Chapter 22.190. This program does not apply inside city limits; if your property is in Halvern or Ardenmoor, contact that city.

## Requirements

1. Pre-application research request ($75) so staff can pull the parcel's permit history.
2. Application on Form PDD-88 with the legalization fee of $610, existing-condition plans, and a site plan stamped by a licensed surveyor where the parcel exceeds one acre.
3. **Onsite wastewater evaluation.** Parcels on septic must submit an evaluation by a qualified professional showing the system has capacity for the additional unit. Environmental Health review of the evaluation is a separate submittal and is the most common source of delay.
4. Fire district clearance for parcels in a high fire hazard severity zone, including defensible space verification.
5. Building plan review and inspection.
6. Issuance of a Certificate of Legal Nonconforming Status.

## Timeline

Typical processing is 90 to 120 days. Files requiring a new septic design routinely exceed six months. The county does not offer a third-party inspection option.

## Corrections now have an executable entry point

[effective_evidence.py](../pipeline/procbench/effective_evidence.py) validates
source-bound, itemized review decisions and materializes a corrected evidence
object without editing the original ledger. Every measurement is accounted for
as admitted, residual-only, held, duplicate, or out of scope. Canonical evidence
and document hashes prevent silently applying a review to a different input.

[compile_visit_durations.py](../pipeline/procbench/compile_visit_durations.py)
accepts `effective_review` and `review_sidecar_bindings` (CLI:

## Typical ranges: implemented, but not silently made into medians

[typical_range_reports.py](../pipeline/procbench/typical_range_reports.py)
models a bounded phrase such as “usually three to five weeks” as interval
censoring of **one noisy subjective population-quantile report**. A declared
finite prior over its quantile is integrated once. The endpoints are not two
quantiles or a confidence interval; the phrase is not relabeled a sample median.
The contract requires `quantiles`, `weights`, `report_log_scale`, an explicit
description, and kind `interval_censored_subjective_quantile`.

This is an opt-in reporting assumption, not a calibrated default. An independent
review corrected the initial scale-field name: the Student-t scale is not its
standard deviation. Individual cases, finite-cohort summaries, disjunctive
intervals and unclear bounds are not admitted through this shortcut. Scalar and
batched V6 paths, including weighted case paths, share the implementation.

### Histogram mechanics in V6

[cohort_histograms.py](../pipeline/procbench/cohort_histograms.py) supplies an
exact normalized multinomial for one declared independent cohort. It requires
integer counts summing to the observed sample size, complete right-closed bins,
calendar-day units, source provenance, clock, population, IID/non-overlap
declarations, and one elapsed-operator mixture. A same-cohort mean may not be
multiplied as independent evidence. Suppressed and unknown counts are rejected.

The integrated [V6 model](../pipeline/procbench/bayes_trace_hierarchy.py) first
forms the weighted, optionally path-conditioned population and then bins it.
It marginalizes a **single interpretation for the entire histogram**. Exact
administrative counts receive no narrative-report scale, contamination or
source-family offset. Increasing the cohort size does not allocate additional
case latents. Cohort identities are checked against other cohorts, individual
claims and correction-rate observations. No new architecture was introduced.

This remains an experimental finite-grid likelihood. Hard bin assignment is
discontinuous and positive counts with zero modeled mass have zero probability;
no pseudocount conceals that problem. The precision gate requires a genuinely
larger **effective** population grid. Independent review caught the 257 → 258
configuration loophole: both round to the same 512-point Sobol grid. The gate now
reports actual grid sizes and refuses to certify that as refinement. Prediction-
only refinement and chain extension cannot silently change this likelihood.
Real CalFresh compilation/fits remain blocked on source semantics, reporting
selection and numerical validation.

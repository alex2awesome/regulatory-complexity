## What is still needed for the paper's stronger claim

The current results support a documented benchmark and qualified evidence of
reported applicant friction. They do **not yet** demonstrate a unified model
that learns heterogeneous-source bias, internal agency effects, total applicant
effort, or a complete cross-jurisdiction burden ranking. Remaining source use
is tracked in the [forum integration work order](2026-09-20-calfresh-forum-model-integration.md).
The next integration target is shared case/event histories and explicit uncertain
actor assignment, not summing the seven component medians. Unmatched forum and
administrative clocks remain useful for different estimands; their disagreement
alone is not an estimate of source trust.

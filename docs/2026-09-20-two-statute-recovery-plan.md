`frozen documents -> reviewed evidence actually consumed -> V6 -> diagnostics -> matched scoring`

The deliverable is a per-jurisdiction timing estimate with uncertainty and an
auditable structural/fee profile, compared with same-document baselines. It is
not completion of all 489 synthetic processes or the entire physical corpus.

## What the audit establishes, and what remains a hypothesis

Accepted findings:

- The archived numerical results and quotation/hash checks reproduce. They do
  not establish semantic correctness or independence.
- Important whole-process reports are residual-only, especially all such C29
  reports. Whole-process evidence plus selection is the next joint test.
- Review sidecars must change the effective input or block compilation; merely
  linking them in a snapshot does not resolve the errors.
- Revision records need measurement-level before/after edits. Measurement and
  document identities need a namespace before cross-ledger combination.
- The compact synthetic bundle has ten nonpassing documents, but **29 artifacts
  under five hold reasons**. Passing verification does not override a hold.

Qualifications that affect the experiment:

- Twelve label errors in a stratified sample of 60 is not a population error
  rate, nor a measured rate specifically for whole-process claims. Review every
  headline-driving row in the pilot instead of extrapolating that percentage.
- A source-bias term cannot identify optimism, selection and population time
  from two disagreeing sources alone. Administrative anchors, calibration and
  prior sensitivity are required. Improvement is a hypothesis, not guaranteed.
- A typical range is not automatically a median, a cohort confidence interval,
  a hard support bound or two independent observations. Its report model must
  state what it assumes and must not invent a sample size.
- SolarTRACE's old rankings and spread are not validated: the saved fits have
  input-definition problems and failed diagnostics even for operation sums.
  The operation-centred sampler addresses mixing, not duplicate data or clocks.
- Unreported step times and applicant hours are not separately measured by a
  posterior driven by a process total. They stay out of headlines. New external
  measurements could change that limit; it is not a universal impossibility.
- Differences across jurisdictions are not automatically causal agency effects
  or effects of adding a requirement. Case mix, policy era and selection matter.

### 1. Freeze a small, fit-usable evidence view

For each selected jurisdiction and each headline-driving claim, review the full
source context, not only the matched quotation. Record:

- source edition/date, jurisdiction, policy era, initial/renewal/amendment route;
- case/episode identity, copies and correlated updates;
- start/end events, completion/censoring, report date versus event date;
- individual experience versus forecast, requirement, typical range or cohort
  statistic; units and rounding; the actual meaning of any stated n;
- issuer versus operator/provider/payee; fee scope and overlap; required inputs
  versus distinct operations or actual encounters.

Every selected row needs a disposition: informative, residual-only, duplicated,
out-of-scope, or unresolved/held. Keep originals, quotes and excluded rows in the
audit archive. Build a separate effective input containing adopted corrections
and dependency decisions, with an itemised old/new field diff and source support.
Use compound identities `(ledger namespace, local measurement ID)` and
`(source edition/hash, local document ID)`; namespacing is not deduplication.

Unresolved mandatory holds fail closed. A cross-ledger copy is not two cases.
One applicant's overlapping total and segment reports are not independent
population draws. A compiler report must show each row's effective disposition
and the likelihood/operator that consumes it, or why it does not inform timing.
Do not retroactively fill missing historical access declarations with `false`.

**Gate:** every pilot whole-process timing, headline count and fee-total row has
a source-bound review and an effective disposition; zero unresolved mandatory
holds silently enter the likelihood; originals and changes revalidate by hash.

### 2. Close the existing C29/C44 diagnostic, once

Use the same frozen document edition for V6 and both baselines. Do not drop a
long complaint or an abandoned case because its inclusion hurts recovery.
Review the new combined input independently of the development target.

Implement within V6, default-off until tested:

1. An explicit observation likelihood for typical-range reports, with declared
   report uncertainty and unknown cohort size. Do not relabel them medians.
2. Quoted, route-specific event bindings. Preparation wording can map to model
   start only when that start actually represents preparation. Vague "whole
   thing" retains supported alternatives rather than a universal start alias.
3. Forum selection with whole-case clocks actually informative, respecting
   episode outcomes, right-censoring and the population normalizer.
4. A source-type reporting-bias layer with uncertainty, anchored or calibrated
   outside scored targets. Generic per-document zero-mean offsets already exist;
   adding an agency term must not double-count the same reporting shift.

Freeze a small comparison grid before outcomes are opened: existing control,
whole-clock repair, whole-clock plus selection, and the combined source-bias
model. Controls already run can be reused only where source/input hashes match.
No growing grid of task-prior or graph sensitivities after inspecting scores.

Compare with (a) a frozen rule selecting the source's typical-range sentence
and reporting its midpoint as a descriptive baseline, and (b) three independent
LLM runs on the identical documents and target definition. The sentence baseline
does not acquire a confidence interval by treating its range as one.

**Gate:** numeric diagnostics pass and each development median is within
0.7–1.4 of its matched truth clock with 90% interval coverage. Also report absolute
log error, interval score, evidence coverage, runtime and prior sensitivity.
Passing these two already-examined programs is development recovery, not held-out
validation or evidence of Bayesian superiority. If they fail, diagnose once and
report the failure; do not restart an open-ended architecture search.

### 3. V6 at the resolution the selected sources support

Reuse V6's measurement and hierarchy machinery. Represent only identifiable
aggregate phases or whole-process clocks; a phase is not a primitive task.
Do not manufacture a detailed handoff or serial order from a list of documents.
Timing-only fits do not sample unobserved labor channels. Use the operation-centred
sampler where applicable, with a small timing probe before a production run.
The objective is minute-scale pilots; that is a performance target to measure,
not a promise that coarsening guarantees speed.

For solar, start from the corrected phase panel: deduplicate owner/year/size/
technology/phase cells, retain business-day semantics and qualified same-day
zeros, preserve real utility identities and unknown denominators. **Do not sum
phase medians into a total.** Fit permit timing and PTO only when their respective
clocks have directly supporting evidence or an explicit joint timing model.

For CCW, start with initial applications at the best-covered accepted authorities;
audit independent case counts before accepting the historical "14 authorities
with ten cases" cutoff. Freeze a common policy era or explicitly model eras.
Training, Live Scan, issuer processing and permit pickup have distinct operators
and endpoints. Renewal and amendment evidence is retained separately.

Share semantic phase/task categories across jurisdictions, not durations or
operation instances merely because their names match. Estimate hierarchy spread
only where the crossed design and evidence identify it; keep source bias and
case mix distinct. A proper posterior alone is not identification.

### 4. Small matched synthetic study, then real fits

Solar uses a frozen, verified subset of S07 rather than rendering all 57 county
implementations now. CCW uses a **new world/version**, not changes to W2's twelve
statutes. Draft a bounded pilot (initially at most twelve jurisdictions), with
calibration/development/reserved jurisdictions declared before generation. The
exact case counts, document quotas and partitions must be frozen before rendering.

The CCW design needs policy-era-specific rules, explicit application/interview/
background/training/decision/pickup clocks, multiple reporting channels, shared
agency/task factors where meaningful, non-completion and forum selection. Include
controlled missing step information and copied reports. It must test the model,
not reproduce the same likelihood under a different filename: include at least
one misspecified reporting/selection condition and source-poor jurisdictions.

Separate construction/private truth, source-only render/parse packets, inference
inputs and evaluator outputs by directory and saved access briefs. Fits and
baselines see only the frozen public edition. Every score binds world and
manifest revision, not just a world name. Any old reserved labels already seen
by a coordinator are not newly held-out evidence.

Test clock recovery, interval calibration, missing-source behavior and aggregate
jurisdiction spread. Match the spread estimand to the generator: a planted county
effect is not automatically the model's agency-effect SD. The existing 0.37
benchmark is a scored target, never a fitted prior chosen to recover itself.
Twelve jurisdictions may give a wide scale interval; disclose that rather than
using coverage alone as proof of accurate spread recovery.

Only after semantic, numeric and synthetic gates pass, fit the selected real
solar and CCW panels. A real result has no synthetic truth score; validate against
held-out administrative summaries/cases when endpoint and population match.

## Headline outputs and stopping rule

Headlines: matched elapsed clock with uncertainty; documented applicant-facing
requirements and conditional step inventory; applicable schedule fees with scope.
Separate these from expected realized steps, repeated encounters, actual spending
and lost income, which need additional evidence. Applicant/agency hours and hidden
step times stay non-headline unless directly supported and identified.

Freeze baseline prompts, source selection, seeds and metrics before evaluation.
Point accuracy, interval quality, replicability and pooling/transfer are separate
claims. If V6 does not outperform the matched LLM on timing in the frozen study,
report that result. The contribution becomes the benchmark, calibrated evidence
representation and cross-jurisdiction structural analysis—not an unsupported
claim of superior clock estimation. Do not change the primary metric afterward.

## What parsing means

Read the entire retained post/comment tree once. One unit is an author's
installation, with that author's updates linked; a thread is not a case, and
three snippets from the same comment are not three cases. A general advice
comment, legal deadline, installer forecast, and completed individual duration
are different observation types. Neighbor/friend stories are retained as
secondhand, not silently promoted to independent first-person installations.

Exact source hashes and JSON-string quote locators are checked mechanically
once. This catches extraction errors; it does not establish semantic truth.
Cheap workers handle capture, rendering and mechanical bookkeeping. Statistical
and ambiguous semantic decisions remain with the main agent or Sol. Early
Luna keyword-window packets (`parse/batch001.json`, `parse/cross_program.json`)
are **candidate discovery only, not model-ready episodes**.

The first main-agent semantic batch is `parse/root_decisions001.json` (ten
timing-rich threads). Its explicit decisions distinguish final-inspection to
PTO from final-documents submission to PTO, and installer handoff delay from
utility review. It also catches a two-year billing activation wait which is
not a two-year PTO delay. This batch is not the full corpus or representative
of all installations. Unread threads remain queued, not recorded as zero.

## Bayesian integration

`pipeline/procbench/process_structure/solar_forum_bridge.py` compiles reviewed
whole-episode checklists into the existing uncertain-jurisdiction occupancy
model. It uses an 11-action AHJ block, preserving the original 50-ID registry
as metadata. It is a posterior over documented action availability, not a
posterior over the number of encounters experienced by every applicant. Some
administrative actions are nested; their count must not be called applicant
burden. Unreported actions are narrative nondetections, not known absence.

Contextual candidate weights are declared assumptions. Broadly located cases
are retained with broad candidate sets; exact department names are unnecessary.
Where the panel's serving-utility crosswalk is implausibly broad, use an
explicit broad-California prior rather than a fabricated precise assignment.
Location inferred from a reply's context is flagged separately from direct
self-location. The geographic frame is conditional on California and the
panel's 440 authorities; it does not cover every California authority.

Time measurements retain their units, clock boundaries, intervals, and
unfinished-case censoring. Unspecified-start PTO reports remain usable for
their own reported-wait estimand, not forcibly matched to an administrative
phase. The source-trust note remains binding: forum/administrative differences
cannot be called source bias unless they compare the same clock/population.
Install counts are not the sample size behind published medians.

An initial bridge using positive-only timing extractions ran as a diagnostic.
It is **invalid for primary inference**: positive selection was not corrected
in the likelihood and convergence failed (R-hat 1.0345, ESS 101). The explicit
warning is in `model_bridge/production_fit_v1/INVALID_FOR_PRIMARY_INFERENCE.md`.

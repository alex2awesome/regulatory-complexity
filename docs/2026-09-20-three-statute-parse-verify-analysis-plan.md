## 1. Objective and completion boundary

Deliver one reproducible evidence-to-results chain for each family:

`frozen sources -> complete extraction -> effective reviewed evidence -> V6 -> validation -> comparison`

Answer three separate questions: can we faithfully recover what documents say;
can the Bayesian system recover burden under realistic reporting mechanisms;
and does pooling heterogeneous evidence improve prediction, uncertainty or
replicability over same-information baselines?

“Fully” means **every relevant source in a frozen on-disk inventory is accounted
for**, not every possible source on the live internet. Inventory the entire
existing corpus for relevance to these three families, including previously
withheld and unassigned captures. Read all relevant frozen documents, complete
threads and workbook contents; record duplicate representations, unreadable
sources, missing attachments and exclusions explicitly. An unresolved relevant
source prevents a claim of complete readable-source coverage. Missing live-web
material remains a named coverage limitation, not a quietly completed task.

Full extraction and phased analysis are different deliverables. Start small
audited fits while completing the three-family parse. Do not stop parsing after
the pilot, but do not wait for thousands of forum pages to test the compiler.
No new queues for unrelated programs, all 489 W2 processes, or broad retrieval.
Any essential missing-source retrieval gets a separately bounded gap list.

These are three **program families**, not literally three isolated statutory
sections. Their policy eras, local rules and implementing authorities matter.

For all three, distinguish a documented requirement, a primitive operation, an
aggregate phase, an applicant encounter and repeated work. Report documented
step inventories and alternative inventories separately from expected realized
step counts. Silence is neither zero steps nor evidence of parallel execution.
Use an LLM to reconcile semantic identities with source support; preserve
uncertain merges and routes as explicit alternatives rather than forced matches.
Agency identities are categorical over a versioned organization register with an
unresolved/out-of-chart option; an issuer, operator, payee and provider need not
be the same organization.

Applicant hours, agency labor hours, hidden step durations and lost income are
non-headline unless directly supported and identified. Waiting time does not
measure staff effort. CalFresh benefit payments are transfers, not administrative
fees or negative burden. Do not rank these three families by incomparable clocks
or invent a scalar burden index with unagreed weights.

## 5. Verify semantics and materialize the effective evidence

Use the same procedure on all three real and all three synthetic corpora.

1. **Mechanical verification, every record:** input/output hashes, contract
   validation, exact normalized quote re-slicing, unit/currency/date arithmetic,
   source edition, complete coverage and native workbook coordinates. OCR-only
   values require source-image checks. Record missing historical access
   declarations as unknown; do not invent them after the fact.
2. **Semantic verification, every potentially informative record:** source-bound
   review of statistic, population, clock, route, outcome/censoring, actor, step
   identity, payment scope and dependencies. Review whole-process statements,
   count/fee headlines, case links and source-bias anchors independently a second
   time. For tables, independently verify each distinct field/cohort definition
   and every anomaly; deterministic replay verifies the bulk cells.
3. **Audit recall as well as precision:** blind re-extraction from sampled
   complete sources, not just checks of already extracted quotes. Before looking
   at results, sample up to 100 non-headline claims and 20 source units per family,
   stratified across source types; use a census if smaller. Report weighted
   omission/label error estimates and uncertainty, separately by class. These
   sample checks do not certify every nonsampled reading.
4. **Escalation:** any wrong headline value/clock or duplicated independent case
   blocks the affected release. A repeated systematic error, or over 5% observed
   material error in an audited stratum, triggers a full affected-stratum review
   and a fresh audit sample. Five percent is a review trigger, not an assertion
   that 100 checks prove an error rate below it.
5. **Effective input:** adopt corrections into a versioned derived ledger with
   measurement-level old/new diffs, supporting quotes and reviewer decisions.
   Apply mandatory holds before compilation. Namespace IDs before joining, then
   deduplicate sources and episodes; namespacing alone does not remove copies.
   Keep originals and uncertain alternatives. Emit a row-to-likelihood report,
   including every retained-but-unused claim and the reason it is not consumed.

LLM reconciliation handles language and proposed identities; the model handles
uncertainty that survives calibrated semantic review. Measure top-reading
accuracy by ambiguity class on labeled calibration material. Roughly 90% is the
prior audit's screening heuristic, not a universal guarantee. For weaker classes,
compare calibrated candidate mixtures with top-reading controls on both timing
and counts. Correlated model votes are not independent expert labels. Refresh
these checks for each new domain/world; do not expose reserved labels to parsers.

## 6. One Bayesian architecture, with appropriate observation types

Extend **integrated V6**, not a fourth parallel architecture. The shared objects
are jurisdiction/organization identities, coarse process inventories, cases or
cohorts, source families and typed observations. Family-specific files define
clocks, routes, field crosswalks and policy eras, not separate inference engines.

Required compiler/likelihood work, in order:

1. Consume effective review dispositions and reject unresolved mandatory holds.
2. Admit whole-process and typical-range reports with explicit report uncertainty;
   a typical range is neither two observations nor automatically a confidence
   interval. Bind start/end phrases using reviewed route-specific evidence.
3. Support aggregate phase medians with unknown precision when n is unknown,
   and monthly cohort counts, means and histograms with their dependence declared.
   Initially use one justified summary per cohort and reserve the other for a
   predictive check; a later joint likelihood must account for shared cases.
   Unknown n is not a reason to invent precision or discard all the information.
4. Combine whole-clock evidence with forum selection and reporting-source bias.
   Administrative counts/means are not intrinsically unbiased anchors for a
   different population. Calibrate outside evaluation targets; show sensitivity
   where selection, reporting bias and population time cannot be separated.
   Avoid double-counting existing per-source offsets with new source-type shifts.
   Incorporate the [source-trust proposal](2026-09-20-source-trust-note-for-codex.md),
   with the implementation qualifications below.
5. Add only hierarchy dimensions supported by the crossed design: shared task
   categories across jurisdictions, actual agencies/utilities, explicit era/time
   effects where needed. Do not equate all operation instances of one task.

### Source heterogeneity: ordered implementation and identification gates

Alex added the source-trust proposal to the execution scope on September 20.
Treat reporting bias, reporting precision, contamination and case/institution
selection as separate quantities, not a single “trust” score. Do not rank Reddit,
agency self-reporting and oversight a priori. No naturalisation or unemployment
parse is added by their mention as possible future calibration settings.

1. **Measure support first.** Every preflight and posterior report must show
   source-family candidate rows, residual-only rows, distinct cases and independent
   cohorts; reporting scales; and prior/posterior offsets where allocated.
   Exact administrative histograms have no narrative-source offset. An allocated
   coordinate with zero offset-sensitive evidence is not a learned source effect.
   Operator candidates alone do not certify structural support or identification.
2. **Bind comparable estimands.** Freeze reviewed comparison blocks over program,
   jurisdiction, policy/time window, population/ascertainment, clock endpoints,
   statistic and cohort lineage. Merely pairing boundary strings is not an
   identifying design. Individual durations and population medians can share an
   underlying distribution without estimating the same report statistic.
   The [executable comparison screen](2026-09-20-source-comparison-contract.md)
   now validates declared ordinary-summary blocks and reports reduced-design
   rank/replication; it does not itself certify a review or estimate source bias.
3. **Precision before extra hierarchy.** Use the finite-cohort likelihood only
   when a reviewed denominator describes the reported cases and the sampling,
   statistic and dependence assumptions are explicit. Use source-type reporting
   scales calibrated separately from cohort variation. A large n reduces sampling
   uncertainty, not systematic error or selection. On a clean matched fixture,
   increasing n should concentrate the cohort statistic toward its population
   value; an aggregate need not universally outweigh an individual report when
   its systematic uncertainty or coarsening is larger.
   The [implementation and controlled influence check](2026-09-20-source-precision-and-cohort-handoff.md)
   now cover fixed optional type scales and the structural-bridge cohort handoff;
   neither is empirical source-type calibration.
4. **Reference-anchored relative bias.** Add one reference-type convention and
   non-reference type contrasts with explicitly centered document residuals
   nested within type. Fixing only a type mean while leaving document means free
   retains a location ridge; declare constraints and prior-predictive meaning.
   Reference zero is a coordinate/anchor assumption, not proof of unbiasedness.
   Widen relative-effect priors through calibration/sensitivity, not by fitting
   a preferred development truth. A narrow Gaussian penalizes large shifts; it
   does not make them impossible. Learn type/document hyper-scales only when
   crossed support is sufficient; otherwise carry declared calibrated scales.
5. **Source-specific contamination, cautiously.** Add per-type rates with
   calibrated shrinkage and intervals only after testing their separation from
   case variation, reporting scale and selection. In sparse strata, report prior
   dominance and run fixed-rate sensitivity. An oversight institution-selection
   mechanism is not the current forum case-selection mechanism.
   Include a clean but genuinely heterogeneous-jurisdiction negative control:
   stronger pooling must not earn apparent robustness merely by routing correct
   extreme reports into a broad-error component. Report that allocation
   separately from any claim of source incorrectness. The
   [first solar sensitivity sweep](2026-09-20-solar-coarse-pilot.md) demonstrates
   why this check matters; it does not calibrate the mixture.
6. **Explicit non-timing policy and transport.** Report that count observations
   currently share one error scale/contamination rate and no source offset; this
   is a limitation, not equal credibility. Keep published fee schedules distinct
   from noisy reported totals. Learn cross-type contrasts within reviewed solar
   (and any genuinely comparable CalFresh) coverage, then test transport to CCW
   as an assumption with a no-transport control. Do not assert CCW is identified
   from its forum volume alone.

Source-model acceptance requires relative-bias recovery on crossed calibration
fixtures, intervals and predictive checks under no-overlap/prior-dominated
fixtures, source-block and derived-contrast chain diagnostics, and matched
held-out log/interval scores. A forced posterior shift or a smaller interval is
not itself success. No production defaults change merely to make source offsets
move away from zero.

Start with a few measured phases or a whole-process location/spread. Do not
sample unobserved labor channels or create detailed case latents for aggregate
rows. Integrate nuisance case effects where accurate and cheaper. Before fitting,
report design rank/confounding and prior-predictive behavior; after fitting,
report prior/posterior sensitivity, marginal variance contraction and uncertainty
in the actual reported estimands. Variance contraction alone is not “fraction of
information learned,” and small marginal contraction can hide learned sums.
Prior-determined components stay out of headline agency/step claims.

Use multiple MCMC chains and the operation-centred sampler where appropriate;
document numerical integration separately from posterior sampling. Default pilot
release checks: rank-normalized R-hat <= 1.01, bulk/tail ESS >= 400 for primary
estimands, stable Monte Carlo error, and existing integration/refinement checks
including derived operation sums. Freeze exact tolerances before scoring. A
proper posterior, a completed job or good coordinate R-hat alone does not pass.

Run parser/compiler and short CPU probes locally. Benchmark one representative
fit before selecting local versus sk3 capacity; use sk3 for larger multi-chain
jobs when available. Record wall time, CPU-hours, dimensions and peak memory.
No GPU is assumed necessary. Run all stages from one family-configured driver
and shared manifests/scorers; avoid another collection of per-experiment scripts.

### Development order

whole-clock repair, plus selection, plus reporting bias. These are two already

smoke test, not evidence of calibrated coverage from two examples. Diagnose one

In parallel finish the shared compiler and family evidence packets. Pilot the
corrected solar phases first, CalFresh cohorts next, and CCW clocks as reviewed
thread packets become available. A source-faithful reduced model can pass a
family-specific gate even if a more elaborate selection decomposition remains
unidentified; label exactly what it does and does not estimate.

### Baselines and tests

Freeze an evaluation protocol before opening reserved outcomes:

- **Direct-source baseline:** the declared typical-range sentence when available;
  otherwise the matching published statistic or a predeclared empirical case
  estimator. Report no estimate when unsupported. A quoted range is not promoted
  into a confidence interval. Use no performance-based sentence selection.
- **LLM baseline:** three independent runs using identical frozen public inputs,
  clock/population questions and output schema. Large corpora use a deterministic
  chunking/aggregation budget with complete source accounting, not hidden
  truncation. Give both methods the same cross-jurisdiction information.
- **V6:** the frozen integrated model, plus a small predeclared ablation set:
  no pooling, pooled, pooled plus heterogeneous reporting/selection. Run a
  same-reviewed-ledger comparison as well as the source-to-answer comparison,
  separating extraction quality from aggregation quality.
- **Evidence accumulation:** fixed, nested source bundles—official material;
  official plus administrative aggregates where available; all channels including
  narratives. Also predeclare random-thinning levels of 10%, 25%, 50% and 100%
  by independent source/case family, not by copied document. Measure where more
  information helps, saturates or introduces reporting bias. Do not render
  thousands of documents just to create redundant observations.

Primary timing metrics are absolute log error of the matched posterior median
and a proper 90% interval score; also report coverage and interval width.
Never treat missing baseline uncertainty as a zero-width calibrated interval.
Report fit/abstention failures in the denominator, not just surviving cases.
Aggregate by jurisdiction/family with cluster-aware uncertainty across worlds;
30 repeated worlds give uncertain coverage estimates, not exact certification.

Score structural inventories, actor assignments, routes and fees separately:
precision/recall, count error/interval coverage, scope-correct fee error and
abstention. Step-identity hypotheses use calibrated weights rather than treating
every LLM mention as a new step. Score hierarchy spread and held-out-jurisdiction
prediction separately from single-jurisdiction timing. Report runtime, token
usage, sensitivity and variation across parser/baseline runs and MCMC seeds.
Repeat extraction on a frozen audit subset: a stable sampler cannot eliminate
upstream LLM variability by itself.

### Real-world release

After relevant semantic, numerical and synthetic gates pass, analyze all
eligible observed real jurisdictions/eras, with an explicit exclusion/coverage
table. Do not restrict final analysis to the pilot's best-covered authorities.
Use blocked time or held-out-jurisdiction/source-family predictions where data
permit; no copied reports or dependent summaries cross the split. Administrative
figures used for fitting cannot also serve as independent validation targets.

Release per-jurisdiction clock distributions, documented requirement/agency
inventories and scoped costs, with evidence strength and prior sensitivity.
Compare structural variation with timing while controlling available era/case-
mix covariates. This is observational association, not the causal burden of a
particular statutory step or a proven “intrinsic agency speed.” Agency-level
posteriors can still be useful, but their conditioning and identification must
be explicit. No synthetic recovery score substitutes for real-world validation.

If V6 fails to improve timing, uncertainty or transfer over the matched LLM,
report that result and retain the benchmark/evidence/structural contribution.
Do not move the primary metric after looking, or promise that more documents
must make the hierarchy identifiable.

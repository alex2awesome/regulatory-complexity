## What still needs work

The 1,352 uncertain representations include 756 empty query captures, 197
image/visual-review holds and 179 sparse/partial text representations. These are
physical states, not independent missing documents: another representation may
already contain the source. An empty query result does not demonstrate that no
discussion exists. Failed captures also occur among candidate-relevant paths;
candidate status does not mean a successful read.

The [row-level recovery accounting](../runs/three_family_20260920/relevance_screen_017/recovery_state_closeout.json)
keeps nonexclusive indicators separate from mutually exclusive totals. A global
count of useful primary logical documents remains unset: PDFs, extracted text,
metadata, copied reports and distinct empty requests must not be summed as
independent evidence. Further source reads should first join candidates to
existing full-read packets and deduplicate their lineages.

The next scientific bottleneck is reviewed measurement compatibility, not file
count. Recent [solar oversight reads](../runs/three_family_20260920/solar_oversight_fullread_001/README.md)
and [CalFresh oversight reads](../runs/three_family_20260920/calfresh_oversight_fullread_001/README.md)
found useful context but no admissible empirical application-time observations.
The [fixed-48 concealed-carry descriptive packet](../runs/three_family_20260920/ccw_324060_fixed48_finite_descriptive_v1/README.md)
has actual reported clocks, but endpoint variants, policy eras, within-person
dependence and voluntary reporting still constrain pooling and interpretation.

No new retrieval queue was opened to finish this census. Study execution,
remaining synthetic verification and model-admission work continue under the
[three-family plan](2026-09-20-three-statute-parse-verify-analysis-plan.md).

# CCW licensing in 15 small and mid-size California counties

Collected 2026-09-16 by a scoping agent. **Preserved verbatim from the agent's
report**: it was a sub-agent of the 58-county CCW sweep, which died on a session
rate limit before it could write its own file, so this is the only copy. The
other nine scoping workstreams did not run; see
[the scale-up plan](../2026-09-16-county-scale-up-plan.md) §2 for what they were
to cover.

Statute: Penal Code §26150–26225 as amended by SB 2 (2023), with the §26205
90/120-day decision clock. Issuing authority: the county sheriff (and some city
police chiefs).

## Method and caveats, as reported

Every fact below came from a page the agent actually fetched (sheriff sites,
official Permitium portals, policy PDFs, annual reports) or from a URL that
appeared in search results. County web servers for **Shasta, Madera, Kings,
Sutter, San Benito and Tuolumne** returned 403/Cloudflare challenges to every
fetch method; for those the sheriff's Permitium "start" page (authored by the
sheriff) is the primary source. The search budget ran out after the first
discovery pass, so news, grand jury and statistics items were pursued only by
direct fetches. **"Not verified" means nothing was found by those means, not
that nothing exists.** No per-county DOJ CCW statistics page was located.

## Cross-county observations, as reported

- **Permitium in 13 of 15.** Lake and Siskiyou are paper and in person only.
- **Published Lexipol policy** found for Napa (207), Nevada (207), Tehama (218),
  Siskiyou (218). All four make the psychological evaluation discretionary;
  Siskiyou caps it at $150. Mendocino says "may be required." No county in this
  set states a mandatory evaluation for all applicants.
- **Explicit processing times**: Nevada "3–6 months"; Tuolumne "minimum of
  twenty (20) weeks" for the DOJ/FBI return; Humboldt "within 120 days";
  Lake 4–12 weeks before training; Calaveras (2015) 10–12 weeks. Everyone else
  says "multiple weeks", which is the Permitium boilerplate.
- **Three character references** is universal among the Permitium counties.
  Siskiyou requires three signed letters from people known five years or more.
  Lake is silent.
- **Fee spread for a new standard licence**: Siskiyou $118 to Lake $549. The
  Lake figure includes a sheriff-run course, which is itself a process
  difference: Lake is the only county in the set that trains its own applicants.
- **City programs**: Madera PD runs its own. Napa PD is winding down (no new
  applications after 1/1/2026, renewals through 12/31/2026). Tehama excludes
  Red Bluff and Corning residents. Siskiyou and Calaveras refer city residents
  to city chiefs. Humboldt and Nevada explicitly cover all cities in the county.
- **Per-county counts do exist in sheriff annual reports**, not in a central
  dataset: Nevada 2024 (796 permits processed, 625 new issued, 1,225 valid,
  374 Live Scans); Tehama 2022–2025 (initials 165/154/139/96, renewals
  775/856/524/719, Live Scans 340/545/641/591); Napa 2021 (196 new or renewed,
  746 Live Scans).

## What this already settles for the benchmark design

1. **Process variation is real and structural, not cosmetic.** Channel (portal
   versus paper), who trains (outside vendor versus the sheriff), who issues
   (sheriff versus city chief), whether a psychological evaluation can be
   demanded, and the fee by a factor of 4.6. These are exactly the
   jurisdiction-level "readings" and route choices the synthetic decision layer
   plants.
2. **The declared tier is thin and unevenly thin.** Four of fifteen counties
   publish a policy document; several link to documents that 404 or sit behind
   Cloudflare. A published fee table and a published policy disagree in
   Mendocino. This is the availability model, observed.
3. **Timing evidence is mostly boilerplate.** Eleven of fifteen say only
   "multiple weeks", which is vendor text, not a county statement. Four give a
   real number, and those four are the counties with a published policy. So
   disclosure of timing correlates with disclosure of process, which the
   generator should reproduce rather than treat as independent draws.
4. **Rural counties are not information-free, they are information-different.**
   Annual reports carry permit counts for small counties (Tehama, Nevada) where
   no dashboard exists. Local news carries launch stories with occasional
   numbers. This supports harvesting annual reports and grand jury reports as
   the rural-coverage channel, per the plan's §2 A9.
5. **403 and Cloudflare walls are a first-class retrieval failure mode** that
   the mock web does not yet model. Six of fifteen county sites blocked a
   scripted fetch entirely, which is a realistic and currently unmodelled
   ceiling on any agent's retrieval.

## Requested parsing subagent

Once the count gate is met, spawn the explicitly requested parsing subagent
with **no conversation-history fork**: this conversation contains development
truth and audit values. Supply public artifacts and the evidence contract,
prompt and validators only. Its job is extraction/reconciliation, not scoring.
The existing entry point is `pipeline.procbench.reconcile_evidence`; preserve
raw attempts, model/prompt/schema/source hashes, exact quotes and failures.

Require the following distinctions:

- County, city, administering agency and program service area are distinct.
  Identically named departments in different counties are not one agency.
- Inventory alternatives retain uncertain steps, actor identity, route order
  and repeat structure. Missing dependencies do not imply parallel work.
- Same case, copied claim and overlapping cohort are different links; preserve
  multiple claims and their distinct endpoints, not one observation per document.
- Elapsed cycle, waiting, service, payer hours, agency hours and money remain
  separate. Normative deadlines and quoted forecasts are not realized durations.
- Pending cases, partial traces, incomplete counts and ambiguous endpoints
  remain in the ledger. A value unsupported by the model is not zero burden.

Never use generator inventory to fill a missing source-derived route or org
chart. Do not present uncalibrated LLM confidence/vote counts as independent
Bayesian evidence. Respect calibration/development/reserved boundaries; any
label-review job is separate and explicitly records permitted truth access.

## Readiness inspection while awaiting the count gate

Read-only code inspection on September 17 confirms these current boundaries;
it is not a claim that a W2 parser or fit ran:

- `VisitHierarchy` consumes `programs`, each with explicit `routes`,
  `observations` and optional `rate_observations`. A route carries an ID,
  positive declared prior weight, operations and sequential blocks. An
  operation has actor identity/role, task type, optional event markers and
  true/false/unknown payer/agency-work applicability. `edges: null` defaults
  to serial order; `edges: []` explicitly permits parallel work. Do not turn
  missing evidence into the latter.
- `process-evidence/2` does **not** have a count measurement metric. It
  preserves activities and relations, but numerical visit/agency/rework counts
  need a quote-checked sidecar or a versioned contract extension. Do not encode
  these as `annual_volume` or silently lose them in the elapsed compiler.
- V6 observations require distinct IDs, source families, case/cohort distinction,
  interval bounds, a statistic, an explicit sampling assumption and supported
  operators with normalized positive interpretation weights. Same-case claims
  must share the sampling mechanism. Actor IDs are pooled literally, so agency
  namespace reconciliation across counties/programs matters.
- A population observation with `sample_size` is currently rejected: finite-
  cohort duration uncertainty is not implemented. Population count summaries
  and selected-population duration summaries are also unsupported. Preserve
  the supplied denominator and coverage disposition; never delete `n` merely
  to make a fit run. The supported correction-rate cohort must explicitly be
  independent, counted and scoped to completed cases for its repeat block.
- The current layout still allocates all three resource channels and per-case
  coordinates. Conditional channel pruning and general case marginalization
  must not be represented as already implemented. Measure dimension/memory
  and distinguish prior-only labor outputs in the requested preflight.
- Those channels are elapsed days, payer hours and agency hours. The trace
  operator validator has no monetary-fee likelihood. Preserve fees, contractor
  charges and staffing quantities in the evidence ledger and coverage report;
  do not turn dollars, drilling hours or billable hours into payer/agency labor
  merely to fit the supported interface. A V6 timing/count run is not a full
  monetary-burden fit.

These observations come from `evidence_contract.py`, `compile_visit_durations.py`,
`compact_visit_trace.py`, `bayes_trace_hierarchy.py` and `run_visit_hierarchy.py`.

## Role of deferred synthetic work

Synthetic data permits known-truth tests of extraction, recovery, interval
coverage and deliberate reporting bias. It cannot establish that real agencies
or forums follow those mechanisms. It is useful validation, not a prerequisite
for source-faithful real descriptive inference, predictive checking or a
carefully qualified empirical paper. Revisit its scope only after the real-data
deliverables above; do not let it remain the critical path.
